# Product lens
Score: 6/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Compelling consumer end-state:** The core promise—authentic, certified consumer electronics delivered in under 2 hours with an immediate 12-month local replacement warranty—solves the primary emotional friction in Dakar tech retail: pervasive fear of counterfeit hardware and non-existent post-sale support.
- **Frictionless native conversion touchpoints:** Offering a 1-click WhatsApp instant checkout paired directly with Wave and Orange Money meets Dakar consumers inside their daily communication flow, removing multi-step registration friction.
- **Craft-conscious technology foundation:** Building a custom lightweight PHP 8.1, Vanilla JS, and Tailwind CSS stack delivers sub-second mobile page loads without unnecessary runtime bloat, reflecting care for local network performance constraints.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **The marketplace identity split (lack of focus and refusal to say no):** Attempting to operate both a curated, direct-retail service with 2-hour delivery and 12-month warranties alongside an open B2B SaaS merchant marketplace (15,000 to 35,000 XOF/month + 4% take rate) dilutes the product identity. Third-party merchants will introduce unverified SKUs, broken dispatch timelines, and inconsistent packaging, destroying customer trust in the SenTechPLUS brand.
2. **Doorstep transaction friction from Cash on Delivery:** Combining Cash on Delivery with SMS OTP verification introduces significant seams into what should be a smooth delivery experience. Couriers face delays counting physical currency, handling change, waiting for SMS codes, and absorbing failed deliveries during peak Dakar traffic, degrading courier velocity and compromising the under-2-hour promise.
3. **An undefined reverse-logistics and warranty experience:** Promising a 12-month local replacement warranty without owning and designing the exact return/exchange handoff will cause catastrophic post-purchase failure. If a device fails in month three and the customer faces friction, inspection disputes, or courier delays, the entire brand promise of certified reliability breaks down at the moment of truth.

## The questions I would ask the founder
1. What is the business in one sentence, without using the words "SaaS", "merchants", or "marketplace"?
2. What does the first five minutes feel like for the customer, from the moment the courier arrives at their door to the moment the product is unboxed and powered on?
3. If a customer purchases an item listed by a third-party merchant on the SaaS tier and it fails after 45 days, who handles the replacement unit, who pays for it, and how is it resolved in under 2 hours?
4. What would happen if you eliminated Cash on Delivery entirely and required 100% prepayment via Wave and Orange Money before dispatch?
5. What are the top 10 hero SKUs that you can deliver with absolute perfection before expanding to five distinct hardware categories?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Kill the B2B SaaS merchant tier completely:** Eliminate merchant storefront subscriptions and third-party fulfillment slips. SenTechPLUS must retain 100% ownership over sourcing, authentication, stocking, packaging, and dispatch so that every customer experience is uniform and controlled.
2. **Narrow the catalog to high-frequency hero SKUs:** Cut the broad five-category catalog down to a focused selection of high-demand, high-turnover accessories (such as GaN fast chargers and certified audio) where authenticity and immediate 2-hour delivery provide an undeniable advantage.
3. **Design the full physical unboxing and warranty journey:** Establish strict craft standards for physical touchpoints: sealed, branded tamper-evident packaging, standardized courier handover guidelines, and a frictionless one-touch WhatsApp replacement workflow for warranty claims.
4. **Prioritize mobile money over Cash on Delivery:** Make Wave and Orange Money the default, frictionless payment rails to eliminate doorstep cash-handling friction, driver safety risks, and delivery abort rates.
