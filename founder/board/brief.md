# Board Briefing: SenTechPLUS

## 1. Executive Summary of the Idea
SenTechPLUS is an e-commerce and SaaS marketplace platform operating in Dakar, Senegal. It provides genuine, certified consumer electronics and high-tech accessories (ANC headphones, wireless earbuds, smartwatches, GaN chargers, Bluetooth speakers) with a 12-month local replacement warranty, delivered in under 2 hours via dedicated motorcycle couriers. Payments are integrated natively with local mobile money (Wave, Orange Money) and Cash on Delivery.

Alongside direct retail, SenTechPLUS offers physical Dakar electronics merchants a SaaS storefront subscription (15,000 to 35,000 XOF/month + 4% commission) with automated dispatch slips and order tracking.

## 2. Target Market & Geography
- **Primary Market**: Dakar metropolitan area (1.2M+ urban consumers with mobile money penetration >80%).
- **Primary Audience**: Urban professionals, tech workers, digital creators, students with disposable income seeking reliability, authentic brand certifications, and speed.
- **Merchant Audience**: Verified brick-and-mortar tech retailers in Dakar seeking instant digitisation without building custom e-commerce infrastructure.

## 3. Product Catalog, Pricing & Unit Economics
- **Core Consumer Products**:
  - ANC Headphones: 25,000 – 85,000 XOF (~$40 – $140 USD)
  - Wireless Earbuds: 12,000 – 45,000 XOF (~$20 – $75 USD)
  - AMOLED Smartwatches: 22,000 – 95,000 XOF (~$35 – $155 USD)
  - GaN & MagSafe Fast Chargers: 10,000 – 35,000 XOF (~$16 – $58 USD)
  - Portable Bluetooth Speakers: 18,000 – 120,000 XOF (~$30 – $200 USD)
  - Estimated Average Order Value (AOV): 32,000 XOF (~$52 USD)
  - Gross Margin Target: 35% – 50% on direct retail.
- **SaaS Marketplace Revenue**:
  - Standard Merchant Tier: 15,000 XOF/month (up to 50 active SKUs) + 4% take rate
  - Pro Merchant Tier: 35,000 XOF/month (unlimited SKUs, priority placement) + 4% take rate

## 4. Channels & Operations
- **Fulfillment**: Under 2-hour motorcycle courier dispatch in central Dakar neighborhoods (Point E, Plateau, Almadies, Mermoz, Yoff, Ouakam, Sacré-Cœur). 24h–48h for regional Senegal.
- **Conversion & Discovery**: Direct web platform, 1-Click WhatsApp Instant Checkout, social commerce (TikTok, Instagram, WhatsApp status), local SEO.
- **Payment Collection**: Wave API, Orange Money API, Cash on Delivery with SMS OTP verification.

## 5. Technical & Operational Status
- Technology stack: Custom lightweight PHP 8.1+ / MySQL / Vanilla JS / Tailwind CSS (no heavy runtime overhead, sub-second loads).
- Key operational dependencies: Courier fleet coordination during peak Dakar traffic, working capital requirements for buffer stock, and fraud mitigation on Cash on Delivery orders.
